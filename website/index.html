    Game day, decoded.

    Every new volleyball parent asks why the players keep moving. When her team wins the serve back, everyone rotates one spot clockwise, and whoever lands in spot 1 serves. Try it.

    • Best two of three sets. Sets 1 and 2 go to 25, capped at 27. Set 3 goes to 15, capped at 17.
    • Rally scoring. Every rally earns someone a point. Win by two, up to the cap.
    • Five-in-a-row rule. After five straight points by one server, the serve changes.
    • Equal playing time. No player sits out more than one rotation in a set.
    • Six a side. Four players are needed to start, with a 15-minute grace period.

    Jersey 1 is in spot 1, so she serves.

    The sideline standard

    • Cheer positively for her team, and for good plays by either team.
    • Let the coach coach. Instructions from the stands confuse players.
    • Leave the officials alone. Everyone makes mistakes, officials included.
    • Wait 24 hours before raising a game-day concern with the coach.
    • Be on time at both ends. Drop off no more than 15 minutes early, and pick up within 15 minutes of the end.
