THE CSA WAY

More than a game.

CSA Volleyball is built around learning, teamwork, sportsmanship, and belonging. Players develop their skills at a pace that fits their grade and experience.

01

Learn the fundamentals

Serving, passing, setting, movement, and game awareness build throughout the season.

03

Love the game

Practices and games give every player room to improve, compete, and have fun.

FALL + SPRING

A season with a plan.

The program handbooks describe a nine-week season with one practice per week and an end-of-season tournament. Coaches use a progression that moves from fundamentals into team play.

FOR FAMILIES

Know before you go.

The handbooks are the detailed reference. Here are the essentials families ask about most.

Where are practices?

Program materials identify Comeaux Sports Complex for practices. Confirm your team’s assigned location and time with CSA each season.

Where are games?

Program materials identify Earl K. Long Gym on the University of Louisiana at Lafayette campus for games. Follow CSA’s current schedule for venue details.

What should players bring?

A water bottle, athletic clothing, and court appropriate shoes. Check the player and parent handbook for uniforms, equipment, and facility expectations.

What about rules and safety?

Rules vary by grade. The master handbook covers serving, rotation, conduct, media, and emergency procedures. Coaches and families should review it together.
