Coaching Commitments

By initialing each item below, you confirm your understanding and agreement with each commitment.

InitialCommitment
I will arrive at least 15 minutes before every scheduled practice and game to set up and prepare.
I will remain at the facility until every player has been picked up by a parent or guardian.
I will follow the CSA season curriculum and practice plan framework for my grade division.
I will ensure equal playing time for all players. No player will sit out more than one rotation per set without a documented reason.
I will maintain a positive, encouraging coaching environment at all times. I will not yell at, belittle, or publicly embarrass any player.
I understand and will follow the Two-Deep Leadership Rule: I will never be alone with a single player. At least two adults must be present at all times.
I will not communicate with players via personal social media, direct messaging, or personal text messages outside of official team channels.
I will report any player injury, incident, or concern to the Volleyball Director promptly — the same day if possible.
I will follow the communication ladder: concerns go Coach → Director → CSA Administration. I will not encourage parents to bypass this chain.
I will not argue with officials during games. I will address any officiating concerns calmly and through the Volleyball Director.
I understand that my role is developmental and recreational. Winning is secondary to player development, safety, and enjoyment.

Code of Conduct — Non-Negotiable Standards

The following behaviors constitute immediate grounds for removal from the coaching role, pending review by the Volleyball Director. No warnings will be issued for these violations.

  • Physical contact with players beyond age-appropriate, instructional demonstration. When in doubt, demonstrate on yourself.
  • Any form of verbal abuse, public humiliation, or demeaning language directed at a player, regardless of the player's behavior or performance.
  • Profane, sexual, or discriminatory language in the presence of players or at any CSA event.
  • One-on-one contact with a player outside of the team setting without another adult present (violation of Two-Deep Rule).
  • Private communication with players via personal social media, direct messaging, or text.
  • Alcohol or substance use before or during any CSA volleyball event.
  • Retaliation against a player, parent, or official who raises a concern or complaint.
  • Failure to report a known or suspected incident of child abuse or misconduct.

Sideline Conduct During Games

  • Coaches must remain on the bench side of the court and may not cross the 10-foot line toward the net during play.
  • A maximum of one head coach and one assistant coach may stand on the sideline. Additional coaches must remain in the spectator area.
  • Coaching instructions must be delivered calmly and constructively. No screaming, no negative body language directed at players.
  • Coaches are responsible for managing parent behavior on the sideline. If a parent is violating the spectator code of conduct, the coach must address it — or notify the Volleyball Director.

Handling Difficult Situations
