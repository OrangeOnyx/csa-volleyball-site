Week 5 · Development phase · 5th–6th grade · 60 minutes

Pass, set, hit

Connecting the three contacts · Downball hitting · Court awareness

From the Season Curriculum & Practice Plan Guide by Stephanie Abdalla, Volleyball Director

Net
7′ 0″
Ball
Volley-Lite or standard
Serve
Underhand or overhand, from the end line
Warm-up 15 min Serving 10 Skill 15 Team play 20

  1. Warm-up & ball control 15 min

    • 3-person groups: player 1 passes to player 2 (setter), player 2 sets to player 3, player 3 catches — rotate positions.
    • Focus: passer calls “mine,” setter calls “set,” hitter calls “hit” — build communication habits.

    Swap in from her drill library: Toss–Pass–Set–Catch (3rd grade and up)

  2. Serving 10 min

    • Serve from the grade-appropriate line; focus on serving to specific zones (deep corners, short).
    • Overhand serve from the end line — track accuracy (aim for the back half of the court).

    Swap in from her drill library: Serve and Shag (all grades)

  3. Skill focus: downball & hitting 15 min

    • Introduce downball: standing behind the 10-foot line, toss to self, contact ball with open hand driving it over the net.
    • Introduce the 3-step approach: right-left-right (for right-handers), jump, reach, contact.
    • Key cue: “Reach high, contact the top of the ball, follow through.”

  4. Team play 20 min

    • Game with a rule: every team must attempt at least two contacts before sending the ball over.
    • Award a bonus point for any successful 3-contact sequence.
    • Coach narrates: “That was a pass — now set it — now hit it!” to reinforce the sequence.

The rule for every drill: Every player should touch the ball as many times as possible in every practice. Maximize repetitions. Minimize lines. If players are standing still for more than 60 seconds, restructure the drill.

The bonus point for 3-contact sequences is a powerful motivator. Players will start to understand WHY the sequence matters — not just that they’re supposed to do it.

Coaching tip from Stephanie Abdalla

Recap for parents

Copy this into your team chat after practice.

Week 5 at CSA Volleyball practice (5th–6th grade): we linked pass, set and hit, learned the downball, and started the three-step approach.

Ask her what the passer, setter and hitter each call out: “mine,” “set,” “hit.”
