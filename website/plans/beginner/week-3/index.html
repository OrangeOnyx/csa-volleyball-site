Week 3 · Foundation phase · 1st–2nd grade · 60 minutes

Serve receive basics

Reading the serve · Serve receive positioning · Rotation review

From the Season Curriculum & Practice Plan Guide by Stephanie Abdalla, Volleyball Director

Net
6′ 0″
Ball
Soft Touch or Volley-Lite
Serve
Underhand only, from 10 ft
Warm-up 15 min Serving 10 Skill 15 Team play 20

  1. Warm-up & ball control 15 min

    • Partner passing: increase distance to 15 feet — maintain platform form.
    • Movement drill: coach points left/right, players shuffle; then coach tosses — player passes back.
    • Introduce “ready position”: knees bent, weight forward, hands together, eyes on the ball.

    Swap in from her drill library: Down the Line Passing (all grades)

  2. Serving 10 min

    • Serve from the grade-appropriate line — focus on placement (aim for corners).

  3. Skill focus: serve receive 15 min

    • Introduce basic serve receive: 3 players in a line across the back row.
    • Coach serves (or tosses) from the other side; back-row players call the ball and pass to target.
    • Key concept: the player closest to the ball calls “mine” — others move out of the way.

  4. Team play 20 min

    • Full game with live serving (all grades use the grade-appropriate serve line).
    • Emphasize rotation after every side-out.
    • Debrief: ask players “What position are you in? Who serves next?”

The rule for every drill: Every player should touch the ball as many times as possible in every practice. Maximize repetitions. Minimize lines. If players are standing still for more than 60 seconds, restructure the drill.

By Week 3, players should know their rotation and call “mine” consistently. If rotation is still shaky, spend extra time on it — it is the foundation for everything in Phase 2.

Coaching tip from Stephanie Abdalla

Recap for parents

Copy this into your team chat after practice.
