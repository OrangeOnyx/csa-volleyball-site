Week 8 · Application phase · 1st–2nd grade · 60 minutes

Tournament prep

Tournament rules review · Rotations under pressure · Mental preparation

From the Season Curriculum & Practice Plan Guide by Stephanie Abdalla, Volleyball Director

Net
6′ 0″
Ball
Soft Touch or Volley-Lite
Serve
Underhand only, from 10 ft
Warm-up 15 min Serving 10 Skill 15 Team play 20

  1. Warm-up & ball control 15 min

    • Team warm-up: players lead their own warm-up (coach observes) — builds ownership.
    • Partner serving warm-up: one player serves, partner receives and passes to target.

  2. Serving 10 min

    • Review tournament serving rules for each grade.
    • Each player serves from tournament distance — focus on consistency and in-bounds percentage.

  3. Skill focus: rotation quiz 15 min

    • Put 6 players on the court; call out a score scenario — “We just got a side-out from position 4 — where does everyone move?”
    • Rotate through different scenarios; every player should know their position at any point in the game.
    • Reinforce: “Know where you are. Know who serves next.”

  4. Team play: simulated match 20 min

    • Play a full simulated tournament match: best 2 of 3 sets, full tournament rules.
    • Coach acts as referee — call faults, track score, run time-outs.
    • Debrief: “What did we do well? What do we want to be better at next week?”

The rule for every drill: Every player should touch the ball as many times as possible in every practice. Maximize repetitions. Minimize lines. If players are standing still for more than 60 seconds, restructure the drill.

The rotation quiz is one of the highest-value activities of the season. Players who know their rotation don’t panic in games. Run it fast and make it fun — not a test.

Coaching tip from Stephanie Abdalla

Recap for parents

Copy this into your team chat after practice.
