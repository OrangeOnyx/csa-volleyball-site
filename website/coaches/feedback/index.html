For coaches · week 9

Season feedback

Three questions, about five minutes. Every answer goes to the Volleyball Director and shapes next season’s practice plans.

Draft for the director’s review. These questions are proposed; she may change them before the survey opens.

  1. Which week’s plan ran best, and which one did you change? What did you change?
  2. Which skill did your players find hardest at your grade?
  3. What one thing would make next season’s plans easier to run?

The survey link will be posted here before week 9. Until then, send your answers to the Volleyball Director through CSA’s volleyball page.

No names of players, please. The survey asks about the plans, not about any child.
