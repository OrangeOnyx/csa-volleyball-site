Match Format
  • Best 2 of 3 sets · Sets 1&2 to 25 (cap 27) · Set 3 to 15 (cap 17)
  • Rally scoring — point every rally
  • Win by 2 (until cap)
  • 2 time-outs per team per set · 1 min each
  • 5-in-a-Row Rule: 5 consec. pts → serve changes
  • Coin toss: winner picks serve or side
  • Switch sides each new set · Set 3: switch at 8 pts

Common Faults (Point to Opponent)
  • Ball lands in-bounds on your side
  • Ball sent out of bounds or outside antenna
  • 4 contacts (max is 3)
  • Double contact (same player, twice in a row)
  • Carry / Lift / Throw
  • Net touch while ball is in play
  • Center line violation (interfering with opponent)
  • Rotation fault (wrong position at serve)
  • Serving fault (foot on/over line; ball doesn't cross net)
  • Back-row attack above net height in front of 10-ft line

Side 2 — Conduct & Emergency
🚨 Emergency Protocol
1. Stop play. Clear the court. Stay calm.
2. Assess. Do NOT move player if head/neck injury suspected.
3. Call 911 if serious, unconscious, or unsure.
4. Administer first aid only if trained.
5. Contact parent/guardian using emergency contact on roster.
6. Notify Volleyball Director same day.
7. Document: time, injury, actions, contacts.
Concussion Rule

When in doubt, sit them out.

  • Any head blow → remove from play immediately
  • Symptoms: headache, dizziness, confusion, nausea, light sensitivity
  • No return to play same day. Ever.
  • Written medical clearance required to return

Non-Negotiable Coach Standards

Violation = immediate removal from coaching role

  • No physical contact beyond instructional demonstration
  • No yelling at, belittling, or humiliating players
  • No profane/sexual/discriminatory language
  • No 1-on-1 with a player without another adult present (Two-Deep Rule)
  • No personal DMs/texts/social media with players
  • No alcohol or substance use at any CSA event
  • No retaliation against anyone who raises a concern
  • No failure to report known/suspected child abuse
Sideline Rules
  • Max 1 head coach + 1 assistant on sideline
  • Stay on bench side · Do not cross 10-ft line
  • No arguing with officials — ever
  • Manage parent sideline conduct
  • Deliver coaching calmly and constructively

Player Expectations (Quick)
  • Attend every practice & game; notify coach if absent
  • Arrive ready: proper attire, water, attitude
  • No jewelry (except medical alert)
  • Listen when coach speaks — stop, face, listen
  • Call "mine" before every contact
  • Cheer for teammates on the bench
  • Handshake line after every match — win or lose
Parent Spectator Rules
  • Stay in spectator areas — no court access
  • Cheer positively only — no negative comments
  • Do NOT coach from the stands
  • Do NOT approach officials during/after games
  • No profane or abusive language — zero tolerance
  • 24-Hour Rule: wait 24 hrs before contacting coach about game concerns
  • Pick up within 15 min of session end
